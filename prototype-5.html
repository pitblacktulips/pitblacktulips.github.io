Questions

Good to know.

Anything else? Email appohhey@gmail.com.

Do my friends need the app?

No. Only you use OhHey. It opens WhatsApp, iMessage, Signal, Telegram or your texts to message them.

Does it read my messages?

No. On Android it can notice who messaged you, so it counts as being in touch. It never reads what they said.

Will it nag me?

No. At most one gentle nudge a day, about one friend. You can snooze anyone, or take a break from reminders altogether.

Is it good for people with ADHD?

It was designed with ADHD in mind: friends stay in view, real numbers instead of “a while”, and no streaks or guilt.

What if I get a new phone?

Restore from your own iCloud or Google Drive backup, and everything comes back.
