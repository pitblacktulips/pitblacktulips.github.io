Testing

Thanks for trying OhHey early.

You’re getting a test version before it’s in the App Store and Google Play. It works like the real thing, but you might spot the odd rough edge. That’s exactly what’s useful to hear about.

On an iPhone

  1. Install TestFlight from the App Store. It’s Apple’s app for test versions, and it’s free.
  2. Open the TestFlight link you were sent, on your iPhone.
  3. Tap Accept, then Install. OhHey appears on your home screen like any other app.

New test versions show up in TestFlight, which can update them for you. Test versions stop working after 90 days, by which time there’ll be a newer one.

On an Android phone

  1. Open the download link you were sent, on your phone, and download the file (it ends in .apk).
  2. Tap the file to open it. If your phone asks, allow your browser or Files app to install unknown apps. That’s just because it isn’t from Google Play yet.
  3. Tap Install. If Google Play Protect says it doesn’t recognise the app, tap More details, then Install anyway.

For a newer version, do the same again. It installs over the old one and keeps everything you’ve added.

Your friends stay on your phone

Everything you add stays on your phone, just as it will in the real app. Deleting the test version deletes it too, so if you want to keep it, use Settings → Backup first to save a copy you can restore later.

If the app crashes, it sends a crash report so the problem can be fixed. A report says what went wrong in the code, never anything about you or your friends. You can switch it off in Settings → Privacy. The privacy policy has the details.

Worth trying

  • Add a few close friends, from your contacts or by hand.
  • Log a catch-up when you see someone, and message someone from the app.
  • Try I’m free on the Home tab when you’ve got an evening spare.
  • Add the OhHey widget to your home screen.
  • Not sure what something means? Settings → How OhHey works explains the rings and the rest.

Test versions also have a Testing section at the bottom of Settings. You can ignore it.

Tell us what you think

Anything confusing, broken or missing, or anything you like: message whoever sent you the link, or use Settings → Send feedback, which fills in which version you have. A screenshot helps.

After a week or so, it helps a lot to send Settings → Testing → Usage summary → Share. It’s a short list of how often you used each part of the app, counted on your phone. It has no names, notes or anything you’ve typed, and it only goes to whoever you send it to.

Fixes often arrive by themselves: close OhHey and open it again, and the next time you open it you’ll have the latest.
